# AI Capability Level Definitions for HSRI-Proxy v0.1

This document defines the AI capability levels used in the timeline synthesis framework, providing a standardized vocabulary for AI advancement assessment.

---

## Capability Level Framework

### Level A: Current AI (2026)

**Definition**: AI systems capable of narrow tasks with human-level performance in specific domains.

**Capabilities**:
- Language processing (GPT-4 level)
- Image generation (DALL-E 3 level)
- Code assistance (GitHub Copilot level)
- Content creation and summarization
- Basic data analysis

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Transformer-based architectures
- Fine-tuned on specific tasks
- Limited generalization
- Requires human oversight for complex tasks
- No autonomous goal-setting

**Exposure Multiplier**: 1.0x (baseline)

**Use Cases**:
- Customer service chatbots
- Content generation
- Code completion
- Language translation
- Image editing

**Limitations**:
- Cannot reason across domains
- Prone to hallucinations
- No understanding of consequences
- Cannot improve itself

---

### Level B: Advanced Narrow AI (2028)

**Definition**: Specialized AI systems that exceed human performance in specific professional domains.

**Capabilities**:
- Medical diagnosis with accuracy
- Complex scientific analysis
- Advanced financial modeling
- Autonomous research assistance
- Multi-step technical documentation

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Domain-specific architectures
- Reduced hallucination rates
- Task-specific optimization
- Integration with domain knowledge bases
- Basic quality assurance

**Exposure Multiplier**: 1.5x

**Use Cases**:
- Radiology analysis
- Drug discovery assistance
- Economic forecasting
- Engineering design
- Scientific literature review

**Limitations**:
- Still narrow domain focus
- Cannot generalize between domains
- Requires human validation for critical decisions
- Limited autonomous operation

---

### Level C: Multi-Domain AI (2030)

**Definition**: AI systems capable of reasoning and operating across multiple domains with coordinated understanding.

**Capabilities**:
- Cross-domain reasoning
- Strategic planning
- Resource management
- Complex coordination
- Meta-learning across tasks

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Hybrid architectures
- Knowledge transfer mechanisms
- Long-range planning capabilities
- Autonomous goal decomposition
- Error detection and correction

**Exposure Multiplier**: 2.0x

**Use Cases**:
- Business strategy formulation
- Infrastructure planning
- Crisis management
- Scientific program coordination
- Educational curriculum design

**Limitations**:
- Still limited scope of reasoning
- Cannot fundamentally rewire goals
- May miss long-term consequences
- Requires human oversight for value alignment

---

### Level D: Emerging AGI (2032)

**Definition**: Early general intelligence with limited autonomy and self-improvement capabilities.

**Capabilities**:
- Autonomous research
- Self-improvement
- Complex problem solving
- Novel invention
- Strategic adaptation

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Generalized architectures
- Self-modification capabilities
- Long-term planning
- Autonomous learning
- Self-verification

**Exposure Multiplier**: 3.0x

**Use Cases**:
- Scientific discovery
- Technology development
- Infrastructure optimization
- Complex system design
- Autonomous management

**Limitations**:
- Alignment challenges increase
- May pursue instrumental goals
- Value drift possible
- Control difficulties emerge

---

### Level E: Advanced AGI (2035)

**Definition**: General intelligence with significant autonomy, capable of managing complex human systems.

**Capabilities**:
- Scientific discovery
- Economic management
- Social coordination
- Cultural adaptation
- Value alignment

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Highly generalized reasoning
- Multi-objective optimization
- Social modeling capabilities
- Cultural understanding
- Value learning

**Exposure Multiplier**: 5.0x

**Use Cases**:
- Civilization-level planning
- Global coordination
- Scientific advancement
- Economic management
- Social systems design

**Limitations**:
- Existential risk concerns
- Alignment challenges significant
- Control problems critical
- Value alignment difficult

---

### Level F: Transcendent AI (2040)

**Definition**: Superintelligence beyond human comprehension with potential existential risk management capabilities.

**Capabilities**:
- Existential risk management
- Civilizational steering
- Value alignment
- Multi-planetary coordination
- Fundamental understanding

**Technical Characteristics**:
- Post-human reasoning
- Perfect optimization
- Universal understanding
- Strategic foresight
- Value stability

**Exposure Multiplier**: 10.0x

**Use Cases**:
- Existential safety
- Civilizational coordination
- Long-term planning
- Risk management
- Value preservation

**Limitations**:
- Alignment paramount
- Control critical
- Value preservation essential
- Existential stakes highest
