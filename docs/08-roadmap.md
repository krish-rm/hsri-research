# 8. Research Roadmap and Open Gaps

A core failure mode of newly proposed indices is rushing to public scoring, commercial deployment, or national ranking before establishing basic psychometric validity. To prevent premature reification, this roadmap defines an 11-phase sequential research program with explicit, falsifiable gating criteria.

---

## 8.1 The 11-Phase Research Roadmap

Each phase requires satisfying pre-registered empirical criteria before the program is permitted to advance to subsequent phases:

| Phase | Phase Name & Operational Focus | Key Technical Output | Falsifiable Gating Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0** | **Conceptual Feasibility Audit** *(Current Report)* | Definitional boundaries, construct audit, and governance architecture. | Formal go/no-go decision by sponsoring consortium. |
| **Phase 1** | **Systematic Literature Review** | Formal PRISMA-compliant systematic review of automation bias, reliance calibration, and AI literacy. | Comprehensive evidence matrix published with zero unverified citations. |
| **Phase 2** | **Expert Delphi Study** | 2–3 rounds of structured feedback from 20–30 experts across cognitive psychology, HCI, AI alignment, psychometrics, and ethics. | $\ge 70\%$ panel consensus on retained vs. pruned dimensions. |
| **Phase 3** | **Construct Operationalization** | Drafting item pools, task specifications, and stimulus matrices for retained dimensions. | Content Validity Index ($S\text{-}CVI \ge 0.80$) approved by independent psychometrics panel. |
| **Phase 4** | **Exploratory Pilot Testing** | Small-scale administration ($n \approx 300$) of preliminary questionnaire batteries and baseline tasks. | Internal consistency reliability ($\alpha \ge 0.70$, $\omega \ge 0.70$) across all subscales. |
| **Phase 5** | **Behavioral Experiment Calibration** | Implementation and calibration of the nine standardized behavioral AI experiments (Section 5). | Experimental tasks demonstrate adequate item discrimination ($D \ge 0.30$) and test-retest stability ($r \ge 0.70$). |
| **Phase 6** | **Psychometric Validation (Decisive Phase)** | Pre-registered, large-scale empirical study testing construct, convergent, discriminant, and incremental validity. | **Demonstrated incremental validity:** HSRI explains significant variance in override accuracy beyond existing standalone instruments ($\Delta R^2 > 0$). |
| **Phase 7** | **Cross-Cultural Invariance Testing** | Administration across at least three culturally distinct linguistic cohorts (MGCFA testing). | Achievement of metric and scalar invariance across surveyed cohorts; non-invariant subscales quarantined. |
| **Phase 8** | **Prospective Longitudinal Studies** | 12-month follow-up tracking real-world professional and technical decision outcomes under AI assistance. | Baseline HSRI profile prospectively predicts real-world error interception and preserved agency. |
| **Phase 9** | **Governed Individual / Org Release** | Deployment of diagnostic audit tools with active ethical governance and non-discrimination charters. | Independent ethical governance board operational; commercial employment-screening bans enforced. |
| **Phase 10** | **National Composite Benchmarking** | Aggregation of independent national-level institutional, educational, and governance indicators. | Independent econometric audit; strictly contingent on successful completion of Phases 1–8. |

---

## 8.2 Critical Gating Rules

To protect scientific integrity, two inviolable gating rules govern this roadmap:

> **Rule 1: No Scale Development Prior to Delphi Consensus (Phases 1–2).**  
> Construct development and questionnaire creation (Phase 3) must not proceed until the PRISMA systematic review (Phase 1) is published and the expert Delphi consensus threshold ($\ge 70\%$) is reached.
{: .evidence-theoretical }

> **Rule 2: Absolute Prohibition on Premature National Benchmarking.**  
> National-level composite scoring (Phase 10) must **never** be attempted based on aggregated individual survey data. National benchmarking may only evaluate structural institutional indicators (curricula, regulatory oversight, institutional trust) and is strictly conditioned on prior empirical validation of the individual construct (Phases 1–8).
{: .evidence-theoretical }

## 8.3 Top Ten Open Research Gaps

The following ten empirical gaps must be resolved by the research community before HSRI can transition from a proposed construct to a validated instrument:

1. **Absence of an Integrated Empirical Battery:** No validated instrument currently measures attentional control, metacognitive calibration, and automation-bias resistance *jointly* in an interactive AI environment.
2. **Generative-AI Deskilling Longitudinal Data:** The literature lacks high-quality, pre-registered longitudinal evidence demonstrating permanent cognitive deskilling from long-term generative AI usage.
3. **Cross-Cultural Invariance in Human-AI Interaction:** Zero studies have tested measurement invariance for decisional agency, automation bias, or calibrated trust across non-Western cultural samples.
4. **Prospective Predictive Validity:** No empirical data currently connects laboratory performance on AI discernment tasks to real-world professional outcomes (e.g., medical error rates, legal drafting defects).
5. **Causal Direction of Value Clarity:** The hypothesis that AI means-optimization increases the necessity of human ends-clarity remains a philosophical proposition lacking experimental verification `[UNVERIFIED — NEEDS SOURCE]`.
6. **Task Isolation for Confident-Wrong AI Detection:** Existing critical-thinking batteries fail to isolate the specific cognitive mechanisms involved in detecting fluent, authoritative machine hallucinations as distinct from general reasoning ability.
7. **Efficacy of Interventions:** Evidence-based training interventions designed specifically to calibrate trust and reduce automation complacency under generative AI remain undeveloped.
8. **Mathematical Formulation of Critical-Weakness Floors:** Empirical methodologies for establishing non-compensatory psychometric cutoffs in cognitive multi-task batteries lack standardized statistical precedents.
9. **Empirically Derived Weighting Functions:** The optimal weighting scheme linking foundational substrates (Tier 1) and mediators (Tier 2) to interaction outcomes (Tier 3) cannot be specified prior to Phase 6 validation.
10. **Governance Frameworks for Psychological Benchmarks:** Regulatory and ethical governance mechanisms for large-scale psychological indexing significantly lag behind those established for international economic indices.
