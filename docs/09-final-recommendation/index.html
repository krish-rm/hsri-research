    9. Final Recommendation and Next Experiment

    This section synthesizes the feasibility review into a definitive operational verdict, selects the optimal structural model archetype, and defines the precise next empirical experiment required to test the construct.

    9.1 Definitive Recommendation: Develop as a Research Construct First

    The evaluation of conceptual foundations, psychometric redundancy, measurement architecture, and ethical risks leads to a single, unequivocal conclusion:

    RECOMMENDATION: DEVELOP AS A RESEARCH CONSTRUCT FIRST.

    This recommendation explicitly rejects three alternative courses of action:

    1. Rejection of "Develop a Public Index Now": Launching a public ranking, national leaderboard, or commercial workforce index today is scientifically indefensible. Zero validation data exists, psychometric properties are uncalibrated, and ethical safeguards against employment discrimination and responsibility laundering are unbuilt.
    2. Rejection of "Reject Concept as Wholly Redundant": While all underlying cognitive substrates have validated homes in established psychological literature, existing instruments study these capacities in isolation and outside the interactive context of capable AI systems. A narrow, defensible integration gap exists once the construct is operationalized around behavioral interaction under task asymmetry.
    3. Rejection of Passive "Wait for More Evidence": Resolving construct feasibility cannot be achieved through passive literature monitoring alone. Active, pre-registered empirical experiments are required to determine whether an integrated construct provides incremental predictive validity over separate instruments.

    9.2 Structural Model Archetype Selection

    Three candidate model archetypes were evaluated:

    Model Archetype Structural Composition Methodological Advantages Fatal Flaws & Vulnerabilities Final Disposition
    Archetype 1: Conservative Model Strictly incorporates already-proven psychological instruments (ANT, IUS, Big Five). High initial psychometric validity; zero unverified constructs. Fatal redundancy: Merely re-badges existing cognitive psychology; fails to capture interactive human-AI dynamics. Rejected
    Archetype 2: Integrated Model (Recommended) Anchors foundational substrates (Tier 1–2) in validated instruments while focusing novel measurement on interactive behavioral tasks (Tier 3–4). Isolates the genuine research gap; directly tests behavioral override and calibrated trust under real AI asymmetry; avoids speculative futurology. Requires building and validating new interactive behavioral software environments. Selected & Recommended
    Archetype 3: Future-Oriented Model Introduces speculative constructs designed for hypothetical superintelligence (e.g., recursive self-improvement readiness, cosmic meaning preservation). Broad philosophical scope. High pseudoscience risk: Untestable against observable reality; ungrounded in empirical cognitive science. Rejected

    9.3 Definitive Architectural Summary

    • Operational Definition: The demonstrated behavioral capacity of an individual, organization, or society to maintain calibrated trust, independent judgment, and value-directed goal-setting when interacting with AI systems whose task-specific performance meets or exceeds their own — measured behaviorally wherever possible, not merely self-reported.
    • Core Retained Dimensions:
    • AI-Specific Discernment (Behavioral, Tier 3)
    • Calibrated Trust / Appropriate Reliance (Behavioral, Tier 3)
    • Decisional Agency & Accountability Retention (Outcome, Tier 4)
    • AI Literacy (Imported from UNESCO/OECD/Ng et al., Tier 3)
    • Metacognitive Sensitivity (Methodological Paradigm, Tier 1)
    • Value Clarity (Supplementary Theoretical Dimension, Tier 2)
    • Reporting Rule: Multidimensional profiles featuring non-compensatory critical-weakness flags; prohibition of bare composite rankings.
    • Aggregation Precedent: Non-compensatory dual-cutoff logic inspired by Alkire & Foster (2011) for foundational cognitive thresholds.

    9.4 The Precise Next Empirical Experiment

    To move beyond conceptual debate, the sponsoring consortium must execute a pre-registered, highly focused empirical experiment designed to falsify the construct's claim to novelty:

    Experimental Design

    • Sample: $n = 200\text{--}300$ adult knowledge workers, with sample size determined by an a priori statistical power analysis ($\alpha = 0.05$, power $= 0.80$, detecting small-to-moderate effect size $f^2 \ge 0.10$).
    • Independent Variables & Baseline Measures:
    • Standard Cognitive Reflection Test (CRT-3)
    • Comprehensive Intellectual Humility Scale (CIHS)
    • Validated AI Literacy Questionnaire (Ng et al., 2024)
    • Baseline executive attention (ANT / SART)
    • Experimental Interventions & Tasks:
    • Task A: Confident-Wrong AI Answer Detection Battery (Experiment 1, Section 5).
    • Task B: Cognitive-Forcing-Function Interface Comparison (Experiment 9, Section 5), comparing standard assistance vs. forced-commitment assistance under variable machine confidence.
    • Criterion Outcome: Objective override accuracy on deceptive AI outputs and behavioral reliance calibration.
    • Decisive Statistical Test (Incremental Validity): Hierarchical linear regression and structural equation modeling evaluating: $$\Delta R^2 = R^2_{\text{Full (with AI Discernment Battery)}} - R^2_{\text{Baseline (CRT + Humility + AI Literacy + ANT)}}$$

    Falsifiable Success Criteria

    1. Null Result ($\Delta R^2 = 0$): If existing validated instruments fully account for override performance and reliance calibration, HSRI provides zero incremental validity. Verdict: Abandon HSRI as a redundant construct.
    2. Positive Result ($\Delta R^2 > 0$, statistically significant): If the interactive AI-discernment battery explains significant additional variance in calibrated reliance, empirical distinctiveness is demonstrated. Verdict: Proceed to Phase 2 (Expert Delphi Study).
