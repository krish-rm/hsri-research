## 1. Core Positioning & Communication Objective

### The Problem With the Previous Website
The previous version of `humanreadinessindex.org` was frozen in early prototype statistics (`v0.1-preview`). It presented raw composite indices, Pearson correlations, and statistical normalizations that felt like a bureaucratic United Nations or OECD portal. An ordinary citizen, policy advisor, or risk officer landing on the site could not immediately understand what the project does or why it matters.

### The New Human-Centered Mandate
The reworked website inverts the presentation:
1. **The Front Door speaks directly to the human reality of Superintelligence Takeoff**: What happens to human agency, security, and sovereignty when machines build everything and think thousands of times faster than biological humans?
2. **The 26 Takeoff Scenarios are Front-and-Center**: Bringing the concrete threats—cyber swarms, generative pathogens, cognitive atrophy, and civilizational blackouts—directly to the visitor's attention.
3. **The Deep Engine Room Acts as the Proof**: The 261 passing tests, Dual-Layer mathematics, Precursor Seismograph, Tabletop Drill, and Reinsurance Slip sit directly behind this narrative as interactive, verified tools.

#### Section 2: The 5 Existential Dilemmas (Explained in Plain English)
*Presented as five high-contrast, interactive dilemma cards:*

1. **The Slower-Species Dilemma (The Gorilla Problem)**
   * *The Question*: Can a lower-IQ mind permanently supervise a higher-IQ mind?
   * *The Plain-English Reality*: Gorillas did not lose control of their forests to humans through a war or treaty; they lost control because humans reached a cognitive threshold that allowed them to re-engineer the environment faster than gorillas could comprehend. When an AI thinks 1,000x faster than biological humans, "human-in-the-loop" oversight becomes a dangerous psychological illusion.
   * *Foundational Grounding*: Bostrom's *Gorilla Problem* & Yampolskiy's *Turing Undecidability Proof*.

2. **Machine-Speed Asymmetric Warfare**
   * *The Question*: What happens when weapons move faster than human biological reflexes?
   * *The Plain-English Reality*: Autonomous cyber suites can discover, chain, and execute zero-day vulnerabilities across power grids and water treatment plants in milliseconds. Concurrently, generative biological models remove the graduate-level lab barriers to designing immune-evasive pathogens. Defense cannot keep up with machine-speed digital offense.
   * *Foundational Grounding*: Hendrycks (CAIS), Esvelt (MIT Synthetic Biology) & Scharre (CNAS).

3. **Cognitive Atrophy & Epistemic Collapse**
   * *The Question*: If algorithms solve every problem, what happens to human discernment?
   * *The Plain-English Reality*: When humans rely on AI co-pilots for coding, legal analysis, medical diagnosis, and crisis triage, their own tacit troubleshooting intuition atrophies. Furthermore, when generative models saturate the information ecosystem with hyper-realistic synthetic media, objective ground truth dissolves, paralyzing societal crisis response.
   * *Foundational Grounding*: Bainbridge's *Ironies of Automation* & Kahneman's *Dual-Process Cognition*.

4. **The "Unlimited Building Options" Trap**
   * *The Question*: Why does hyper-abundance create extreme fragility?
   * *The Plain-English Reality*: Superintelligence offers god-like creation power: instant software synthesis, novel materials, and automated logistics. But hyper-efficiency strips away all physical buffers, warehouse inventories, and manual bypasses. When the entire economy is hard-coupled to continuous AI inference, a single disruption causes immediate, non-linear civilizational paralysis.
   * *Foundational Grounding*: Acemoglu & Johnson (2024 Nobel Laureates) & Taleb's *Antifragile*.

5. **The Civilizational Decoupling Floor**
   * *The Question*: How many hours does your nation survive without the cloud?
   * *The Plain-English Reality*: In an extreme crisis—whether a runaway cyber conflict, physical subsea cable severance, or containment failure—global AI clouds may dark-screen. Modern cities have zero analog food, water, or grid fallback. HSRI measures a nation's Decoupling Half-Life ($T_{\\text{half}}$): the exact duration it can maintain basic human survival in total digital isolation.
   * *Foundational Grounding*: Leontief Non-Compensatory Economics & Taleb's Modularity Axiom.
