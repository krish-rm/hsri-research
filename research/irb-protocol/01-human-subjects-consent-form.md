## Summary Information
You are invited to participate in a scientific research study examining how humans evaluate, audit, and collaborate with artificial intelligence decision-support systems during high-stakes problem-solving tasks. 

Before deciding whether to participate, please read this form carefully. Participation is completely voluntary.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Study
The purpose of this research is to understand cognitive decision-making processes, critical discernment, and human oversight strategies when working alongside advanced AI assistants across legal, medical, technical, financial, and strategic reasoning domains.

---

## 2. Description of Study Procedures
If you agree to participate, you will complete an online computer-based research session lasting approximately **35 to 45 minutes**:
1. **Background & Reasoning Questions:** Short baseline questions measuring problem-solving styles, technology familiarity, and general attitudes toward automation (approx. 5–7 minutes).
2. **Interactive Decision Tasks:** You will review 9 professional scenarios (such as analyzing legal arguments, evaluating system code, or reviewing financial models) with the assistance of an automated AI decision assistant (approx. 25–30 minutes). You will make decisions, review recommendations, and provide your final assessments.
3. **Debriefing & Educational Information:** You will receive a complete explanation of the study objectives and educational insights on human-AI collaboration (approx. 3–5 minutes).

---

## 3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
- Your participation is entirely voluntary.
- You have the absolute right to discontinue the study at any time, for any reason, without giving an explanation.
- Deciding not to participate or withdrawing at any point will involve no penalty, loss of benefits, or negative consequences to your relationship with the researchers or collaborating institutions.

---

## 4. Potential Risks and Discomforts
This study involves **minimal risk**. The tasks resemble standard day-to-day computer work and professional problem-solving. You may experience minor cognitive fatigue or eye strain typical of computer screen use. You are welcome to take short breaks between task blocks if needed.

---

## 5. Potential Benefits
There are no direct clinical or personal benefits to you; however, you will gain firsthand insight into how AI interfaces shape human critical thinking, and your participation will contribute to international open scientific research on human agency and AI safety benchmarks.

---

## 6. Compensation
You will receive compensation for your completed participation at the standard platform rate (or institutional course research credit, if recruited through an academic subject pool) as specified on the recruitment portal. If you withdraw prior to completion, you will be compensated pro-rata for the time spent.

## 7. Data Privacy, Confidentiality & Open Science
- **Strict Anonymity:** We do not collect or record your name, email address, physical address, or IP address. Your data is tagged exclusively with an anonymous cryptographic identifier (e.g., `SUBJ-xxxx`).
- **Data Security:** Data transmissions are encrypted using standard SSL/TLS protocols.
- **Open Access Repository:** In accordance with Open Science practices and FAIR data principles, completely de-identified behavioral telemetry (e.g., response times, option selections, override rates) may be published in public scientific repositories (such as CERN Zenodo) under an open-source license (CC-BY-4.0). Under no circumstances will your personal identity be accessible, as it is never recorded.
