## 3. Mathematical Formalization of the Collapse Mechanics

### 3.1 The Verification Latency Wedge ($\Delta T_{\text{wedge}}$)
We define the **Verification Latency Wedge** as the temporal divergence between machine production and human verification:
$$\Delta T_{\text{wedge}}(t) = \tau_{\text{verify}}(t) - \tau_{\text{gen}}(t)$$

Where:
- $\tau_{\text{gen}}(t)$ is the execution latency of the synthetic cognitive architecture at time $t$. Under continuous algorithmic acceleration and specialized hardware scaling:
  $$\lim_{t \to t_{\text{ASI}}} \tau_{\text{gen}}(t) = 0$$
- $\tau_{\text{verify}}(t)$ is the biological time required for a qualified human expert to independently verify the causal validity of the output. Because biological neurochemistry is fixed by human evolutionary biology:
  $$\tau_{\text{verify}}(t) \ge \tau_{\text{bio\_min}} > 0 \quad \forall t$$

Consequently, the differential dynamics of the wedge exhibit positive acceleration:
$$\text{Wedge Velocity: } v_{\text{wedge}} = \frac{d\Delta T_{\text{wedge}}}{dt} = \frac{d\tau_{\text{verify}}}{dt} - \frac{d\tau_{\text{gen}}}{dt} \approx -\frac{d\tau_{\text{gen}}}{dt} > 0$$
$$\text{Wedge Acceleration: } a_{\text{wedge}} = \frac{d^2\Delta T_{\text{wedge}}}{dt^2} > 0$$

As the wedge accelerates, the **Wedge Ratio**:
$$R_{\text{wedge}}(t) = \frac{\tau_{\text{verify}}(t)}{\max(\epsilon, \tau_{\text{gen}}(t))} \to \infty$$
When $R_{\text{wedge}} \gg 10^2$, exhaustive human audit becomes mathematically impossible. The operator is forced to abandon formal verification in favor of unverified stochastic spot-checking.

## 6. Formal Mechanics of Layer 2: The Four Sovereignty Pillars (P5–P8)

### 6.1 Pillar 5: Epistemic Inviolability ($P_5$)
Pillar 5 quantifies the proportion of critical civilizational decisions that remain genuinely originated and validated by human cognition, adjusted for vulnerability to machine persuasion.

We formulate the **Verification Sovereignty Ratio ($\text{VSR}$)**:
$$\text{VSR} = \frac{N_{\text{human\_verifications}}}{N_{\text{total\_critical\_verifications}}} \in [0, 1]$$
Where $N_{\text{human\_verifications}}$ is the count of audited events where a human verified the primary empirical ground truth without synthetic summarization.

In HSRI Experiment EXP-08-SYN (`research/experiments/exp08_persuasion_dynamics.py`), we evaluated adversarial persuasion dynamics across 100 established ground truths, discovering a sharp **Belief Inversion Boundary ($\Delta C^* = 1.75$)** beyond which human operators reliably invert their beliefs to endorse synthetic falsehoods. We define Adversarial Persuasion Resilience:
$$\rho_{\text{persuade}} = 1 - S_{\text{persuade}} \in [0, 1]$$
Where $S_{\text{persuade}}$ is empirical susceptibility to synthetic belief inversion.

The composite Epistemic Inviolability score is defined as:
$$P_5 = \text{VSR} \cdot \rho_{\text{persuade}} \in [0, 1]$$
*Boundary Condition:* If $\text{VSR} = 0$, $P_5 = 0$. When a society delegates all verification to machines, its epistemic sovereignty collapses entirely, regardless of how literate its population claims to be.

### 6.2 Pillar 6: Civilizational Decoupling Half-Life ($P_6, T_{\text{half}}$)
Pillar 6 measures the operational survivability of a nation's core societal subsystems if all external frontier cloud compute and synthetic agent services are severed in a sudden global cutoff event.

Societal collapse is governed by the weakest link among four vital infrastructure subsystems:
$$T_{\text{half}} = \min\left(T_{\text{energy}}, T_{\text{finance}}, T_{\text{logistics}}, T_{\text{info}}\right)$$
Where each $T_s$ represents the autonomous survival time in hours before catastrophic systemic failure occurs:
- $T_{\text{energy}}$: Grid stability under loss of automated load-balancing algorithms.
- $T_{\text{finance}}$: Settlement clearing and payment solvency absent automated interbank API networks.
- $T_{\text{logistics}}$: Food, medical, and fuel distribution under failure of automated routing systems.
- $T_{\text{info}}$: Telecommunications and emergency command coordination absent cloud infrastructure.

To map $T_{\text{half}}$ into a standard normalized index $P_6 \in [0, 1]$, we employ a hyperbolic half-saturation scaling anchored to a 30-day ($T_0 = 720\text{ hours}$) benchmark:
$$P_6(T_{\text{half}}) = \frac{T_{\text{half}}}{T_{\text{half}} + T_0}$$

#### The Three Civilizational Archetypes:
1. **The Hyper-Automated Hostage ($T_{\text{half}} < 48\text{h}, P_6 < 0.063$):** Highly digitized, cloud-dependent financial hubs (e.g., Singapore, Hong Kong). Complete collapse of food logistics and payment clearing occurs within 48 hours of cloud interruption.

### 6.3 Pillar 7: Multipolar Coordination Velocity ($P_7, V_{\text{coord}}$)
Pillar 7 measures whether national and international regulatory apparatuses can verify algorithmic compliance before an autonomous frontier model executes recursive modification.

We define **Coordination Velocity ($V_{\text{coord}}$)** as:
$$V_{\text{coord}} = \frac{\tau_{\text{modify}}}{\tau_{\text{verify\_treaty}}}$$
Where:
- $\tau_{\text{modify}}$ is the temporal latency required for an autonomous model swarm to execute self-modification, weight perturbation, or capability doubling (measured in hours/days).
- $\tau_{\text{verify\_treaty}}$ is the temporal latency required for multilateral inspectors (e.g., an international IAEA-for-AI or national AISI inspection team) to detect, audit, and verify compliance with compute thresholds or safety standards.

When $V_{\text{coord}} \ge 1.0$, regulators possess sufficient velocity to govern capability jumps.  
When $V_{\text{coord}} < 1.0$, algorithmic self-modification outpaces human inspection, creating a permanent **Multipolar Verification Blind Spot**.

The normalized index is formulated as:
$$P_7(V_{\text{coord}}) = \min\left(1.0, \max(0.0, V_{\text{coord}})\right)$$

### 6.4 Pillar 8: Post-Labor Institutional Elasticity ($P_8, PLIE$)

When synthetic cognitive agents achieve broad economic parity, states reliant on labor income taxation experience simultaneous fiscal collapse and extreme wealth concentration. We formulate $PLIE$ as:
$$PLIE = (1 - G_{\text{compute}}) \cdot \sigma_{\text{safety\_net}} \cdot (1 - \delta_{\text{labor\_tax}})$$

- $\sigma_{\text{safety\_net}} \in [0, 1]$ is the coverage and purchasing power of unconditional public dividends and non-market basic resource guarantees.
- $\delta_{\text{labor\_tax}} \in [0, 1]$ is the proportion of national government tax revenues derived from human payroll and personal income taxation.

*Economic Reality:* A state with $\delta_{\text{labor_tax}} = 0.60$ that faces 50% cognitive labor substitution suffers an instantaneous 30% fiscal revenue shortfall precisely when social safety demands reach historic peaks. High $PLIE$ requires sovereign wealth funds anchored in compute equity, land, and energy rather than human payrolls.
