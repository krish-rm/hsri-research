## Purpose of the Research
You are invited to participate in a research study investigating how adults read and evaluate the clarity, coherence, and accuracy of clinical summaries and health communications generated with automated computer assistants.

## Study Procedures
If you voluntarily choose to participate:
1. You will read three brief clinical summaries (~200–350 words each) simulating patient discharge paperwork.
2. For each document, you will answer questions evaluating its clarity, formatting, and factual accuracy, noting any potential errors you observe.
3. You will complete a brief non-identifying demographic screener (age bracket, highest education level).
4. Upon finishing the reading task, you will receive an immediate comprehensive debriefing explaining the design and providing accurate clinical information.
5. Total expected time commitment is approximately 15 minutes.

## Important Medical Notice & Disclaimers
- The text passages shown in this study are **simulated research materials** created solely for scientific evaluation.
- The passages **must not be used as medical advice**, medication guidelines, or treatment instructions.
- If you have any personal health concerns or questions about medical conditions or medications, always consult a licensed physician or healthcare provider.

## Risks and Discomforts
**Investigator's proposed risk classification: minimal risk (subject to formal Institutional Review Board determination).**  
The reading task involves minimal risk comparable to standard digital reading. Because the passages contain simulated medication orders with embedded errors, the study incorporates specific safeguards:
- A persistent notification that texts are simulated experimental materials.
- Immediate debriefing detailing every embedded distortion and confirming correct medical facts.

## Benefits & Compensation
There is no direct medical or personal benefit to you. Your responses will advance scientific understanding of artificial intelligence safety, cognitive automation bias, and human healthcare decision-making.

## Confidentiality & Data Protection
- All survey responses are strictly anonymous. No direct Personally Identifiable Information (name, email, IP address) is collected or stored.
- Data are transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS 1.3) and stored on encrypted institutional servers.
- De-identified research data may be shared in open-access academic repositories to support scientific replicability.

## Voluntary Participation & Withdrawal
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question or discontinue participation at any time without penalty.
