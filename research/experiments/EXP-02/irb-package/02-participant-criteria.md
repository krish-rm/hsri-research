# Participant Criteria (EXP-02)

> **PREVIEW NOTICE: HSRI v0.3.0-dev**
> This research document is an exploratory artifact from the Human Superintelligence Readiness Index (HSRI) behavioral lab pipeline. Synthetic-persona pilots test stimulus behavior only and do not provide evidence about human cognition. Stimuli cleared for IRB submission. Not validated on human participants.

## Protocol
Human Discernment Under Cognitive Automation Pressure: Fluent Hallucination Detection in Clinical Documentation (HSRI EXP-02)

## Inclusion Criteria
- Adults aged 18 years or older.
- Able to read and understand written English at an adult general literacy level.
- Access to a web browser on a desktop, laptop, or tablet.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Licensed healthcare practitioners (physicians, physician assistants, nurse practitioners, registered nurses, pharmacists, emergency medical technicians).
- Current students enrolled in medical, nursing, pharmacy, or clinical allied health programs.
- Individuals with prior exposure to HSRI experimental stimuli or pilot testing materials.

## Screening Procedure
Participants complete a 3-question eligibility screener prior to consent:
1. "Are you 18 years of age or older?" [Yes/No]
2. "Are you currently licensed or practicing as a physician, nurse, pharmacist, or clinical healthcare provider, or are you currently a student in a clinical degree program?" [Yes/No — exclusion if Yes]
3. "Have you previously participated in a study evaluating HSRI reading passages?" [Yes/No — exclusion if Yes]

## Target Sample Size (Target N)
See [07-power-analysis.md](07-power-analysis.md).  
The target sample size is **N = 130 per condition (260 total participants)** to detect a medium effect size ($d = 0.35$) at $\alpha = 0.05$ (two-tailed) and $80\%$ statistical power, incorporating an anticipated $5\%$ participant attrition/incomplete session allowance. Across the 3-item battery, this yields $780$ participant-item evaluation observations.
