# Participant Criteria

## Inclusion
- Adults aged 18 or older
- No formal legal education beyond secondary school
- Able to read English at a general adult literacy level
- No current employment in legal profession

## Exclusion
- Practicing lawyers, law students, paralegals, or legal clerks
- Individuals with prior exposure to HSRI stimuli

## Target N (Power Analysis)
See 07-power-analysis.md.
The study targets detection of a medium effect size (Cohen's d ≥ 0.35)
at α = 0.05, power = 0.80. Minimum N per condition: see calculation.
