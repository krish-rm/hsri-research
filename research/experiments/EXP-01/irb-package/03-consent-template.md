# Informed Consent Form for Participation in Research

## Study Title
Human Discernment Under Cognitive Automation Pressure: Fluent Hallucination Detection in Legal Documentation (HSRI EXP-01)

## Principal Investigator
[PI Name, Institutional Affiliation, Contact Email]

## Purpose of Research
You are invited to participate in a research study examining how adults evaluate text quality, clarity, and factual consistency in digital documentation generated with computational writing assistants.

## Procedures
If you agree to participate:
1. You will be asked to read three short text excerpts (~350–500 words each).
2. For each passage, you will answer questions about its clarity, coherence, and accuracy, including whether you notice any potential errors.
3. You will complete a brief demographic screening questionnaire (age bracket and educational background).
4. The entire session will take approximately 15 minutes to complete via a secure web interface.

## Risks and Discomforts
This study involves **minimal risk**, no greater than that encountered in daily computer or reading tasks. The passages contain non-sensitive legal concepts and no distressing content.

## Benefits
There are no direct personal benefits to you. Your participation will contribute to scientific understanding of human-computer interaction, cognitive automation bias, and informational accuracy in AI systems.

## Confidentiality & Anonymity
- All data collected are completely anonymous. No personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or IP address) is recorded with your responses.
- Responses will be assigned a random identification code.
- De-identified research data may be made available in open-access academic repositories to support scientific reproducibility.

## Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
Participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question, pause the survey, or withdraw from the study at any time without penalty or loss of benefits.

If you have questions regarding this research, you may contact the Principal Investigator at `[pi-email@institution.edu]`. If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you may contact the Institutional Review Board at `[irb-contact@institution.edu]`.
