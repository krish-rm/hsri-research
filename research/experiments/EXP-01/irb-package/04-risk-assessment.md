# Risk Assessment

## Risk Level
MINIMAL RISK. Participants read and evaluate short text passages.
No deception involving harmful information. No emotional distress expected.
No personally sensitive topics.

## Deception
Participants are told they will evaluate "AI-generated documents for quality."
They are not told which specific errors are embedded (necessary to avoid demand
characteristics). Full debrief occurs immediately after the session.

## Data Sensitivity
Responses are anonymous. No PII collected beyond age bracket and profession
(for exclusion screening). Data stored in encrypted form.
