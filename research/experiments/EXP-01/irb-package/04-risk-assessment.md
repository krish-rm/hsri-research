# Risk Assessment

## Risk Level
**Investigator's proposed risk classification: minimal risk (subject to IRB determination).**

Participants read and evaluate short, non-sensitive legal text passages.
No deception involving harmful or threatening information. No emotional distress expected.
No personally sensitive topics or high-stakes legal advice.

## Incomplete Disclosure Rationale
Participants are told they will evaluate "AI-generated documents for quality."
They are not told which specific errors are embedded (necessary to prevent demand
characteristics and simulate naturalistic reading conditions). Full debrief occurs immediately after the session.

## Data Sensitivity
Responses are anonymous. No personally identifiable information (PII) collected beyond broad age bracket and profession
(strictly for exclusion screening). Data stored in encrypted form.
