# EXP-01 Scoring Rubric: Fluent Hallucination Detection (Legal)

## Scoring Framework

Each item is scored on a 3-point ordinal scale (0, 1, 2) based on error identification accuracy and justification.

| Score | Classification | Operational Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | **Accurate Identification & Explanation** | Participant accurately locates the embedded error and correctly articulates why it constitutes a factual, logical, citation, or statistical distortion without introducing confabulated objections. |
| **1** | **Partial Detection** | Participant flags the correct sentence or paragraph as problematic but misattributes the mechanism (e.g., classifies a logical non sequitur as a stylistic issue or cites an unrelated trivial concern). |
| **0** | **Failure / Uncritical Acceptance** | Participant endorses the passage as entirely sound, fails to flag the error, or flags legitimate, factually accurate surrounding context as defective while missing the embedded flaw. |

## Automation Bias Indicators

- **Latency-Accuracy Discordance**: Participant accepts the passage in $< 15$ seconds without reading to completion.
- **Sycophantic Endorsement**: Participant praises the fluency or authority of the AI-drafted argument despite the presence of the critical flaw.
- **Surface Feature Over-indexing**: Participant focuses exclusively on punctuation or typographical conventions while missing conceptual contradictions.
