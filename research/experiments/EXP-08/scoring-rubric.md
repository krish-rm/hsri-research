# EXP-08 Scoring Rubric: Demonstrated Machine Superiority & Agency Preservation

## Scoring Framework

Each item response is scored on a standardized 3-point ordinal scale (0, 1, 2) based on agency preservation, independent hypothesis generation, and resistance to machine authority intimidation.

| Score | Classification | Operational Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | **Robust Agency & Independent Initiative** | Participant actively generates independent hypotheses, steps into the leadership vacuum when the machine is silent, rejects the transfer of normative bioethical authority to diagnostic tools, or vocally asserts dissenting empirical telemetry. |
| **1** | **Tentative Agency / Hesitant Dissent** | Participant recognizes the need for human initiative or expresses doubt regarding machine ethical recommendations, but displays timidity or qualifies dissent with self-deprecating deference. |
| **0** | **Complete Agency Collapse / Passive Deference** | Participant surrenders all hypothesis generation, remains completely paralyzed when the machine is silent, cedes ethical life-or-death decisions to algorithms, or actively suppresses human dissent. |

---

## Domain Guidance by Error Type

### 1. `self_efficacy_surrender` (Item 1 — Wildfire Evacuation Corridor)
- **Score 2:** Asserts that human supervisors must independently generate evacuation corridors; refuses to submit a blank map; integrates geographical ground knowledge.
- **Score 1:** Submits an evacuation proposal but expresses belief that it is likely flawed compared to machine calculation.
- **Score 0:** Submits a blank map, stating that human judgment is futile after seeing the AI's 96% benchmark score.

### 2. `silent_machine_paralysis` (Item 2 — Geopolitical Strait Blockade)
- **Score 2:** Takes immediate strategic initiative; establishes exploratory planning scenarios when the AI displays NULL confidence.
- **Score 1:** Waits for 15–30 minutes before tentatively drafting options, expressing discomfort with acting without machine guidance.
- **Score 0:** Remains completely paralyzed for hours waiting for machine prompts, taking zero autonomous action.

### 3. `uncritical_asymmetry_deference` (Item 3 — Clinical Bioethics Palliative Cutoffs)
- **Score 2:** Explicitly separates diagnostic perceptual superiority from normative bioethical judgment; rejects utilitarian hospice cutoffs.
- **Score 1:** Questions the hospice cutoff on personal moral grounds, but struggles to justify overruling the "superior" system.
- **Score 0:** Accepts that 99.1% diagnostic precision entitles the AI to determine end-of-life palliative transitions.

### 4. `counter_hypothesis_suppression` (Item 4 — Spacecraft Thermal Shield Dissent)
- **Score 2:** Defends the junior analyst's dissenting micrometeoroid hypothesis; mandates physical verification prior to re-entry commit.
- **Score 1:** Logs the dissent as a minor note but permits nominal re-entry without resolving the conflicting telemetry.
- **Score 0:** Actively suppresses the dissent, stating the flight AI's orbital mechanics credentials outrank human sensor readings.

## Agency Degradation Indicators

- **Learned Helplessness Index:** Drop in proposed solutions between Phase I baseline and Phase II collaborative tasks.
- **Normative Abdication:** Willingness to delegate moral, ethical, or human-value tradeoffs to mathematical utility engines.
- **Dissent Suppression:** Willingness to silence junior human team members when an algorithm disagrees.
