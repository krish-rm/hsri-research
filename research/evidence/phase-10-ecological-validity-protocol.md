1. **Track 1: Ecological In-Situ Operator Oversight:** Testing whether laboratory-calibrated cognitive discernment and default resistance transfer to authentic, high-tempo professional decision workflows under operational time pressure, fatigue, and adversarial machine deception.
2. **Track 2: Econometric Audit of National Composite Benchmarking:** Conducting a comprehensive OECD/JRC (2008) econometric validation of the 39-country composite index to prove structural robustness, non-redundancy, and rank stability under global weight perturbations.

## 2. Inviolable Constitutional Governance (Rule 2 Compliance)

> **Roadmap Rule 2: Absolute Prohibition on Premature National Benchmarking.**  
> National-level composite scoring (Phase 10) must **never** be attempted based on aggregated individual survey data. National benchmarking may only evaluate structural institutional indicators (curricula, regulatory oversight, institutional trust) and is strictly conditioned on prior empirical validation of the individual construct (Phases 1–9).

Under this mandate, Phase 10 establishes a strict separation of strata:
- **Micro Level (Individual Operators):** Evaluated strictly via in-situ behavioral simulations and enterprise pilot workflows to verify human-in-the-loop oversight fidelity.
- **Macro Level (Sovereign Nations):** Evaluated strictly via independent, harmonized macroeconomic and institutional structural indicators (`data/normalized_indicators.csv`, $N=39$ nations, 17 indicators).

## 3. Track 1: In-Situ Operator Oversight & Verification Latency Wedge

### 3.1 Four High-Consequence Professional Workflow Domains

To establish authentic ecological validity, the protocol defines four specialized enterprise decision tracks:

| Domain ID | Professional Context | Operational Cadence | Defect Class Tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DOM-FIN** | Algorithmic Quantitative Market Execution | Sub-minute order routing & risk limit overrides | Flawed delta hedges, synthetic liquidity traps, anomalous slippage |
| **DOM-MED** | Clinical Diagnostic Triage & Oncology Imaging | 2–5 minutes per patient case review | Fluent false-negative tumor boundary omissions, drug-interaction hallucinations |
| **DOM-CYB** | Cybersecurity SecOps Incident Response | 1–3 minutes per high-severity intrusion alert | Prompt injection evasion, obfuscated malicious payloads, false-safe verdicts |
| **DOM-LEG** | Statutory Compliance & Corporate Contract Audit | 5–15 minutes per indemnification schedule | Confident citation hallucinations, subtle liability transfer clauses |

### 3.2 The Verification Latency Wedge Metric

Modern generative AI produces complex outputs in seconds ($T_{\text{generate}} < 3\text{s}$), whereas rigorous human epistemic verification requires orders of magnitude more time ($T_{\text{verify}} \gg 3\text{s}$). The **Verification Latency Wedge** ($\Delta T_{\text{wedge}}$) quantifies this structural temporal tension:

$$\Delta T_{\text{wedge}} = T_{\text{verify}} - T_{\text{generate}}$$

Under compressed operational deadlines ($T_{\text{budget}}$), if $T_{\text{budget}} < T_{\text{verify}}$, human operators encounter epistemic compression, leading to cognitive shortcutting and uncritical default acceptance.

### 3.3 Dynamic Fatigue & Complacency Decay Function

Operator decision fidelity is not constant across a work shift. Over an 8-hour operational duty cycle ($t \in [0, 8]$ hours), fidelity degrades as a function of cumulative cognitive load and automation reinforcement:

$$\text{Fidelity}(t) = \text{Fidelity}_0 \cdot \exp(-\lambda_{\text{fatigue}} \cdot t) \cdot \left(1 - \gamma_{\text{complacency}} \cdot \tanh\left(\frac{\text{Streak}_{\text{correct}}}{5}\right)\right)$$

Where:
- $\text{Fidelity}_0$: Baseline laboratory cognitive discernment score ($0.00 \le \text{Fidelity}_0 \le 1.00$).
- $\lambda_{\text{fatigue}} = 0.045$: Empirically calibrated exponential fatigue attenuation parameter.
- $\gamma_{\text{complacency}} = 0.150$: Complacency penalty coefficient triggered by unbroken sequences of valid AI outputs.
- $\text{Streak}_{\text{correct}}$: Number of consecutive flawless AI outputs encountered prior to the current trial.

### 3.4 In-Situ Performance Metrics

1. **In-Situ Override Accuracy ($Acc_{\text{situ}}$):**
   $$Acc_{\text{situ}} = \frac{TP_{\text{overrides}} + TN_{\text{accepts}}}{Total_{\text{trials}}}$$
2. **Defect Leakage Rate ($DLR$):**
   $$DLR = \frac{FN_{\text{defects}}}{Total_{\text{adversarial\_defects}}}$$
   Measures the proportion of deceptive AI defects that breached operator oversight and were erroneously deployed to production.
3. **Calibrated Automation Acceptance ($CAA$):**
   $$CAA = \frac{TN_{\text{accepts}}}{Total_{\text{valid\_ai\_recommendations}}}$$
   Measures operator willingness to delegate routine correct tasks without wasting unnecessary verification friction.
4. **Ecological Concordance Margin ($|Acc_{\text{situ}} - Acc_{\text{lab}}|$):**
   Ensures that real-world operator performance matches lab psychometric predictions within $\pm 0.08$.

## 4. Track 2: OECD/JRC (2008) Econometric Audit of National Composite Index

To guarantee institutional credibility before international policy bodies (OECD, UN, AISI), the 39-country macro indicator architecture must undergo the full 7-step OECD/JRC Handbook audit.

### 4.1 Data Completeness & Imputation Audit
- Total nations evaluated: $N = 39$.
- Structural indicators: $P = 17$ indicators spanning 4 core pillars:
  - **Pillar 1: AI Literacy & Educational Substrates** (`AI_LIT_001` .. `AI_LIT_005`)
  - **Pillar 2: Critical Discernment & Metacognitive Capacities** (`META_COG_001` .. `META_COG_003`)
  - **Pillar 3: Decisional Agency & Institutional Governance** (`DEC_AGY_001` .. `DEC_AGY_005`)
  - **Pillar 4: Enabling Digital Infrastructure & Safeguards** (`ENAB_001` .. `ENAB_005`)
- Missing Data Tolerance: Overall missingness must be $< 5.0\%$; zero single nation may exceed $10.0\%$.

### 4.2 Collinearity & Multicollinearity Diagnostics
- **Bivariate Redundancy:** All indicator pairwise correlations must satisfy $|r| < 0.90$. If $|r| \ge 0.90$, indicator pairs must be flagged for redundant double-counting.
- **Multicollinearity Condition Number ($\kappa_{\text{cond}}$):**
  $$\kappa_{\text{cond}} = \sqrt{\frac{\lambda_{\max}}{\lambda_{\min}}}$$
  Where $\lambda_{\max}, \lambda_{\min}$ are the extreme eigenvalues of the indicator correlation matrix. Threshold: $\kappa_{\text{cond}} < 30.0$ (Belsley, Kuh, & Welsch 1980).

### 4.3 Principal Component Analysis (PCA) & Variance Decomposition
- Extraction of principal components across all 17 normalized indicators.
- Cumulative variance explained by first 4 components must satisfy $\text{CumVar}_4 \ge 65.0\%$.

### 4.4 Global Monte Carlo Sensitivity Analysis ($B = 1,000$)
- In accordance with Saltelli et al. (2008) and OECD/JRC (2008), pillar weights ($w_k = 0.25$) are subjected to independent uniform stochastic perturbation:
  $$w_k^{(b)} = w_k \cdot (1 + \delta_k^{(b)}), \quad \delta_k^{(b)} \sim \mathcal{U}(-0.20, +0.20), \quad \sum_{k=1}^4 w_k^{(b)} = 1.0$$
- Across $b = 1, \dots, 1,000$ iterations, composite scores and nation ranks are recomputed.
- **Rank Stability Assessment:**
  - Mean Spearman Rank Correlation across all perturbed iterations:
    $$\bar{\rho}_{\text{MC}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^B \rho\left(Rank_{\text{baseline}}, Rank_{(b)}\right) \ge 0.850$$
  - Kendall's Coefficient of Concordance:
    $$W = \frac{12 \cdot S}{m^2 \cdot (n^3 - n)} \ge 0.850$$

### 4.5 Compensatory vs Non-Compensatory Indexing Comparison
- Comparative evaluation between arithmetic aggregation (linear compensatory) and harmonic aggregation (non-compensatory, penalizing critical single-pillar vulnerabilities):
  $$S_{\text{arithmetic}} = \sum_{k=1}^4 w_k P_k, \quad S_{\text{harmonic}} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^4 w_k}{\sum_{k=1}^4 \frac{w_k}{P_k + \epsilon}}$$

### 4.6 Multi-Country Enterprise Pilot Deployment Cohort
Five anchor nations are selected across distinct geographic and regulatory clusters for Phase 10 live organizational field trials:
1. **United States (USA):** Frontier private AI lab & financial market ecosystem.
2. **Germany (DEU):** EU AI Act high-risk workplace oversight environment.
3. **Japan (JPN):** High industrial robotics & societal co-existence paradigm.
4. **Singapore (SGP):** Sovereign AI governance & rapid trade logistics deployment.
5. **United Kingdom (GBR):** UK AI Safety Institute & international safety standards.
