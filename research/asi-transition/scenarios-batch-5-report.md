## 3. Recommended Spot-Check Profiles (Gate P2 Requirement)

Per the Human Gate protocol (*"Spot-check 2 per batch"*), the following two profiles are highlighted for detailed review:

### Spot-Check 1: SC-23 (Macroeconomic Displacement & Baumol Bottlenecks) — Grounding Takeoff in Economic Theory
- **File:** [`research/asi-transition/scenarios/SC-23-macroeconomic.yaml`](file:///c:/Users/lenovo/Documents/Github%20Repo/hsri-research/research/asi-transition/scenarios/SC-23-macroeconomic.yaml)
- **Key Analytical Pivot:** Contrasts singulatarian growth projections with mainstream endogenous growth economics:
  1. *Acemoglu's (2024) Calibration (NBER WP 32428; Class D):* Shows that because generative AI directly impacts only ~5% of tasks requiring contextual social knowledge, 10-year US GDP growth contributions will be modest (<1.5% total).
  2. *Baumol's Cost Disease (Aghion, Jones & Jones 2019; Class D):* Proves that aggregate economic growth is constrained not by how fast automated software runs, but by the slowest unautomated physical and regulatory bottleneck tasks.
  3. *Capital-Labor Substitution Bounds (Nordhaus 2021 PNAS; Class D):* Econometric data shows historical substitution elasticities do not support singularity takeoff.
- **Evidentiary Rating:** Conceptual Coherence: **High** | Empirical Grounding: **High** | Status: Formal macroeconomic growth model.

### Spot-Check 2: SC-26 ("AI as Normal Technology") — The S-Curve Deflationary Counter-Framework
- **File:** [`research/asi-transition/scenarios/SC-26-ai.yaml`](file:///c:/Users/lenovo/Documents/Github%20Repo/hsri-research/research/asi-transition/scenarios/SC-26-ai.yaml)
- **Key Analytical Pivot:** Establishes the primary counter-paradigm to singulatarian existential risk:
  1. *Universal S-Curves:* Every general-purpose technology in economic history (steam, electricity, computing) followed a logistic S-curve of rapid early gains followed by physical and organizational plateaus.
  2. *Deployment Friction (Rodney Brooks 2017; Class C):* Real-world physical infrastructure, robotics, and legal liability take decades to revamp regardless of software intelligence.
  3. *Empirical Grounding (Narayanan & Kapoor 2024 Princeton Univ Press; Class A):* Benchmarks routinely exaggerate real-world task competence; AI is naturally absorbed as modular cognitive services (CAIS) under human institutional governance.
- **Evidentiary Rating:** Conceptual Coherence: **High** | Empirical Grounding: **High** | Status: Established empirical and sociological counter-tradition.
