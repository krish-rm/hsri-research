## 5. Methodological & Institutional Takeaways
1. **The Leontief Law of the Minimum**: Even if Power Dispatch is preserved at 60%+, a complete freeze in Food Logistics (5%) pulls the entire society's functional capacity down to 5%. Partial optimization is futile without addressing the weakest link.
2. **Speed of Fracture**: For Hyper-Connected City-States (Archetype A), fractures occur within hours. Mitigations must be pre-scripted into national emergency acts before zero-hour severance.
3. **Decoupling Plateau vs Collapse**: With active analog air-gap equilibria enacted, societies arrest exponential collapse into a survivable analog plateau at 35–65% capacity.
