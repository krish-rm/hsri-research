Data Usage Guidelines

Terms of Use

HSRI data is provided for non-commercial research and educational purposes. When using our data, please adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Cite the Human Superintelligence Readiness Index in all publications
  • Include appropriate methodology documentation
  • Do not redistribute raw data without permission
  • Be aware of data quality limitations
  • Update your analyses when new data becomes available

Citation Format

HSRI Research Team (2026). Human Superintelligence Readiness Index 2026. Human Superintelligence Research Institute. Empirical Benchmark Release v0.1.

Data Quality & Provenance Information

Coverage Scope:39 benchmark nations (OECD, EU, and high-income Asia-Pacific economies; BRICS+ and low/middle-income economies are unrated in this release due to source coverage gaps).
Coverage Status:Unverified preview sample — 39 benchmarked, 59 partial/contextual, 86 unrated in 145-country audit.
Completeness:Exploratory preview sample (780 populated observations across 20 indicators; primary source microdata unverified).
Source Reliability:Derived from World Bank, OECD, ITU, and official statistical publications; subject to survey wave geographic boundaries and imputations.
