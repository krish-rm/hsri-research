Sample Scope & Composition: The current preview benchmark evaluates 39 nations, heavily weighted toward high-income and OECD economies. This reflects a coverage limitation of current international empirical data sources, not a judgment about other nations' readiness. In our coverage assessment of 145 countries, 59 possess partial or contextual data, while 86 evaluated countries currently lack sufficient multi-pillar data and remain unrated.
