The HSRI Epistemic Self-Correction Loop

Unlike traditional static policy benchmarks, HSRI operates a living empirical sentinel architecture:

1. Continuous Scanning

Monitors preprint servers and peer-reviewed journals for new experimental evidence on automation bias, human-AI cognitive synergy, and epistemic verification.

2. Automated Reconciliation

Reconciles newly extracted papers against our 44-claim master evidence table, flagging challenged assumptions as REVIEW_REQUIRED.

3. Multi-Model Epistemic Review

Challenged claims trigger adversarial debates across independent frontier model families, enforcing that no ranking hardens into unverified dogma.
