Methodology

The timeline projections are based on a combination of expert surveys, technological trend analysis, and modeling of AI development trajectories. Each scenario represents a different pathway based on varying assumptions about technological progress, investment levels, and breakthrough potential.

Key Factors Influencing Timeline

  • Research Investment: Global spending on AI research and development
  • Talent Availability: Number of qualified AI researchers and engineers
  • Computing Power: Availability of computational resources
  • Algorithmic Progress: Improvements in AI algorithms and architectures
  • International Cooperation: Level of global coordination on AI safety
