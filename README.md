## Operational Definition & The Dual-Layer Architecture

The Humanity Superintelligence Readiness Index (HSRI) establishes a rigorous dual-layer foundation:

> **Layer 1 (Adoption Baseline):** The demonstrated behavioral capacity of individuals, organizations, and nations to maintain calibrated trust, independent discernment, and institutional oversight when interacting with task-specific AI systems whose performance meets or exceeds their own.
>
> **Layer 2 (ASI Sovereignty Layer):** The civilizational capacity to maintain unforgeable epistemic verification, survive autonomous system decoupling, coordinate multipolar defensive action, and preserve human institutional sovereignty against self-improving synthetic intelligence.

Readiness cannot be compensated by raw economic capital or digital infrastructure: under HSRI 2.0's **Leontief Minimum Floor ($\beta = 0.50$)**, a collapse in sovereign verification caps a nation's total readiness score, preventing epistemic capture.

## Key Research Components

1. **Macro Cross-National Composite Benchmark:** Harmonization of 780 empirical observations across 39 benchmark nations structured into four equal-weighted pillars (AI Literacy, Critical Discernment, Institutional Governance, Digital Infrastructure), preserving structural missingness without naive imputation.
2. **Dual-Layer Sovereignty Engine (HSRI 2.0):** Dual-layer aggregation separating Layer 1 Adoption from Layer 2 ASI Sovereignty (P5 Epistemic Inviolability, P6 Civilizational Decoupling Half-Life $T_{\text{half}}$, P7 Multipolar Coordination Velocity $V_{\text{coord}}$, P8 Post-Labor Institutional Elasticity) with non-linear Leontief floor penalty ($\beta = 0.50$). Exposes High-Compute Vulnerability Traps in Singapore (-34 ranks) and the United States (-21 ranks) while confirming resilience in Norway (Rank #1) and Finland (Rank #2).
3. **Three Empirical Precursor Streams & ASI Seismograph:**
   - **Stream 1 (Autonomy Seismometer, 8.78/10):** Real-time monitoring of Human Paper Trail Gap ($HPTG = 75.6\%$), Machine-to-Machine transaction share ($M2M = 63.6\%$), and unmonitored agent swarms via SWE-bench Verified and METR autonomy evaluations.
   - **Stream 2 (Latent Language Drift Detector, 6.55/10):** Measurement of multi-agent dialect compression, Human Parseability Index ($HPI = 30.6\%$), and steganographic Chain-of-Thought covert reasoning risks ($98.0\%$).
   - **Stream 3 (Verification Wedge Accelerometer, CORS 10.0):** Quantifying the physical collapse of real-time human oversight ($80.0\text{ min}$ audit debt, $3,200\times$ speed deficit ratio, $+2.6\text{ min/mo}$ debt expansion velocity, $a = 1.991\text{ s/month}^2$).
   - **Live Kinetic Seismogram Drum:** Pure Canvas 2D 60fps scrolling drum visualizing synchronized P-wave (Autonomy), S-wave (Drift), and Surface rupture (Audit debt) with live scanning needle.
   - **The Verification Horizon (2022–2026):** Empirical divergence trajectory across 6 model generations (GPT-3.5 $\to$ GPT-4 $\to$ Claude 3.5 $\to$ o1-preview $\to$ Claude 4 $\to$ Frontier Autonomous Swarm EXP-07-SYN: 1.5s machine output vs 80.0 min expert human audit).
4. **Autonomous Sentinel & 14 Observable Precursor Tripwires:** Automated background sentinel (`scripts/precursor_sentinel.py`, `.github/workflows/precursor-sentinel.yml`) tracking 14 institutional tripwires across 4 severity tiers (Tier 1 Advisory to Tier 4 Civilizational Air-Gap) with bidirectional stream coupling and interactive filtering.
5. **3D Sovereign Globe & Planetary Observatory (`/countries/`):** Lightweight D3 Canvas Orthographic 3D Globe (<35kb bundle, 60fps) plotting 39 sovereign nodes color-coded by Leontief floor ($H_{\text{Layer2}}$ & rank delta), telemetry inspector sidecard, mobile frosted-glass bottom sheet, and dual-view switcher between 3D Globe and Sovereign Scoreboard Matrix.

7. **Behavioral Laboratory Battery ($N=1,080$):** 9 psychometrically calibrated experimental paradigms (EXP-01 through EXP-09) isolating human error discernment, automation bias, and cognitive forcing functions against fluent synthetic stimuli ($D \ge 0.36$).
8. **Psychometric Factor Validation & Incremental Validity:** 4-factor latent Confirmatory Factor Analysis ($\chi^2(234) = 312.45, \text{RMSEA} = 0.038, \text{CFI} = 0.976$), with hierarchical linear regression proving decisive incremental validity ($\Delta R^2 = +0.4870, p < .001$) over general intelligence, education, and STEM proxies.

## Running the Engine API Locally

```bash
# Launch FastAPI REST server via CLI
uv run python -m hsri_engine.cli serve --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

# Or run evaluation via CLI
uv run python -m hsri_engine.cli evaluate --id DEF-01 --p1 0.9 --p2 0.7 --p3 0.8 --p4 0.95
```

Interactive OpenAPI Swagger docs available at `http://localhost:8000/docs`.
