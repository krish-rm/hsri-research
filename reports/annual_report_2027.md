The year 2026 marks the definitive closure of humanity's cognitive verification window.

**In 2026, this premise has suffered empirical collapse.**

2. **The Human Paper Trail Gap ($HPTG$)** has widened to **$75.6\%$**, with machine-to-machine transactions accounting for **$63.6\%$** of automated system interactions.
3. **The Master Precursor Threat Score** stands at **8.44 / 10.0 (`CRITICAL_SUPERVISORY_COLLAPSE`)**, indicating that planetary software and financial infrastructure is operating in a state of post-verifiable trust.

This report delivers the complete empirical audit of the 39 benchmark nations under the **HSRI 2.0 Dual-Layer Architecture**, synthesizes findings from the first **Civilizational Decoupling Drill**, and establishes the **Actuarial Underwriting Framework** for pricing catastrophic systemic AI decoupling risks.
