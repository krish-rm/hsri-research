## 1. PIAAC PSTRE Country Participation and Module Administration Audit

**Context:** The preprint previously claimed that six EU nations did not administer the PSTRE module during Round 1 (OECD, 2016). Primary documentation reveals two key discrepancies:
1. `ISL` (Iceland) and `MKD` (North Macedonia) are not European Union member states (Iceland is EFTA/EEA; North Macedonia is an EU candidate).
2. Only Cyprus (`CYP`) participated in PIAAC Cycle 1 (Round 1) and opted out of administering the optional PSTRE module. The remaining five nations (`BGR`, `ISL`, `MLT`, `MKD`, `ROU`) did not participate in the Survey of Adult Skills rounds reported by OECD (2016).

## 2. European Media Literacy Index (EMLI) Geographic Coverage Audit

**Context:** The preprint previously claimed that non-EU nations are not surveyed by EMLI (Lessenski, 2023). However, the primary Open Society Institute – Sofia (OSIS) report covers 41 European countries, explicitly including non-EU European economies.

### Primary Source & Repository Concordance

- **Primary Source:** Lessenski, M. (2023). *Media Literacy Index 2023: Bye-bye, Birdie... Media Literacy Index 2023*. Open Society Institute – Sofia. URL: `https://osis.bg/?p=4492`.
- **Passage Location:** Pages 3–7 ("Methodology and Country Coverage: 41 European countries").
- **Covered Economies in Lessenski (2023):** 41 European nations (all 27 EU member states, plus 14 non-EU European countries: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Iceland, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Norway, Serbia, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine, United Kingdom, and Moldova).
- **HSRI Rated Economies (39 total):**
  - **30 European Economies:** AUT, BEL, BGR, CYP, CZE, DNK, EST, FIN, FRA, DEU, GRC, HUN, IRL, ITA, LVA, LTU, LUX, MLT, NLD, POL, PRT, ROU, SVK, SVN, ESP, SWE (26 EU members), plus GBR, NOR, ISL, MKD (4 non-EU European members).
    - **HSRI Status:** All 30 have **observed** EMLI values (`status: Observed`).
  - **9 Non-European Economies:** AUS, CAN, HKG, ISR, JPN, KOR, NZL, SGP, USA.
    - **HSRI Status:** All 9 are **missing** (`status: Missing`), annotated as `Non-European economy: not surveyed in Council of Europe / OSIS EMLI index`.
  - **Non-EU European Findings:** European non-EU nations (GBR, NOR, ISL, MKD) are fully surveyed in EMLI and have observed values in HSRI. The exclusion applies strictly to non-European economies, not non-EU economies.
