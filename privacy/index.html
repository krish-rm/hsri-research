Privacy & Your Data

Humanity Readiness is designed so anyone can explore breakthroughs, test readiness lights, and check country facts without creating an account or sharing personal details.

1. Local-First Browser Storage

Your preferences, breakthrough reflections, five lights, first-time Toto greeting dismissal (hr:v1:intro_seen), and language settings live locally inside your browser under the hr:v1:* prefix. We do not set cross-site advertising cookies.

2. Crowd Threshold & No PII Rule

Future anonymous aggregate statistics will never display group figures for fewer than 20 independent responses, and any optional free-text breakthrough suggestions stay in a moderated review queue with identifiers removed before publication.

3. Optional Updates & Clear Unsubscribe

Any weekly recap signup is strictly opt-in and consent-based, with one-click unsubscribe built in from day one. Questions? Write to contact@humanityreadiness.org.
