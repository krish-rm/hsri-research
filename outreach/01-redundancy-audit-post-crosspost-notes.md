- **Post Under Real Identity or Consistent Established Handle:** On LessWrong, anonymity or newly generated pseudonyms without a track record often receive skepticism when proposing new evaluation paradigms. Using your real academic/professional identity or an established account signals accountability.

Plan to actively monitor and substantively engage with critique for at least 48 hours post-publication.
